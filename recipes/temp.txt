Sausage, Peppers, Onions, and Potato Bake

This sausage and potato bake is an Italian comfort food classic! The smell of this cooking in my kitchen transports me back to playing on the street in front of my Papa and Gigi’s house in Brooklyn. I make 2 trays of this for every family get-together, and it’s gone in a flash!

Prep Time: 20 mins
Cook Time: 40 mins
Total Time: 1 hr
Servings: 8

Ingredients
- 2 teaspoons olive oil 
- 2 pounds Italian sausage links, cut into 2-inch pieces
- 1/4 cup olive oil 
- 4 large potatoes, peeled and thickly sliced
- 2 large green bell peppers, seeded and cut into wedges
- 2 large red bell peppers, seeded and cut into wedges
- 3 large onions, cut into wedges 
- 1/2 cup white wine  
- 1/2 cup chicken stock  
- 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning 
- salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste

Directions
1. Gather all Ingredients. 
2. Preheat the oven to 400 degrees F (200 degrees C).
3. Heat 2 teaspoons olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat, and cook and stir the sausage until browned, 5 to 10 minutes. Transfer cooked sausage to a large baking dish.
4. Pour 1/4 cup of olive oil into the skillet, and cook potatoes, stirring occasionally, until browned, about 10 minutes.
5. Place the potatoes into the baking dish, leaving some oil in the skillet.
6. Cook and stir green and red peppers and onions in the hot skillet until they are beginning to soften, about 5 minutes.
7. Add the vegetables to the baking dish. Pour wine and chicken stock over the vegetables and sausage, and sprinkle with Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper.
8. Gently stir sausage, potatoes, and vegetables together.
9. Bake in the preheated oven until hot and bubbling, 20 to 25 minutes. Serve hot.

Nutrition Facts (per serving)
- 539 Calories
- 30 Fat 
- 46g Carbs 
- 20g Protein

Healthy Green Juice

This green juice recipe with kale, cucumber, celery, apples, and ginger is great for detoxing after the holidays or whenever you have overdone it! Adjust fruit and veggie amounts to suit your personal taste.

Prep Time: 10 mins 
Total Time: 10 mins
Servings: 2

Ingredients
- 4 stalks celery, leaves removed
- 2 green apples, halved
- 1 cucumber
- 6 leaves kale
- 1/2 lemon, peeled
- 1 (1-inch) piece fresh ginger

Directions
1. Gather all ingredients.
2. Process celery, apples, cucumber, kale, lemon, and ginger through a juicer.
3. Serve immediately or store in a glass jar in the fridge for up to one day; shake before drinking.

Nutrition Facts (per serving)
- 144 Calories
- 1g Fat
- 36g Carbs
- 4g Protein

Best Pork Chop Marinade

This pork chop marinade is savory, slightly sweet, with a little spice, and the best I’ve ever made—I’m so glad I wrote the recipe down! The pork chops taste great grilled and always turn out tender and juicy.

Prep Time: 10 mins
Cook Time: 20 mins
Additional Time: 6 hrs
Total Time: 6 hrs 30 mins
Servings: 2

Ingredients
- 2 large pork chops
- 1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil
- 4 cloves garlic, chopped
- 3 tablespoons dark brown sugar
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice
- 2 tablespoons spicy brown mustard
- 2 teaspoons dried thyme or to taste
- 1 teaspoon onion powder
- 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
- 1 teaspoon white wine vinegar
- 1 teaspoon mesquite-flavored seasoning
- 1/2 teaspoon dried parsley flakes
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt, or to taste
- 1/2 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper

Directions
1. gather all ingredients.
2. Place pork chops on a flat work surface. Slice horizontally through the middle, being careful not to cut all the way through to the other side. Open the two sides and spread them out like an open book.
3. Whisk olive oil, garlic, brown sugar, lemon juice, mustard, thyme, onion powder, Worcestershire sauce, vinegar, mesquite seasoning, parsley, salt, and pepper together in a bowl; pour into a large resealable plastic bag.
4. Add pork chops, coat with marinade, squeeze out excess air, and seal the bag. Marinate in the refrigerator 6 to 8 hours.
5. Preheat an outdoor grill for medium-high heat and lightly oil the grate. Remove pork chops from the marinade and shake off any excess. Discard the remaining marinade, or transfer it to a saucepan and bring it to a boil. Boil for several minutes before using as a sauce.
6. Cook chops on the preheated grill until nicely browned on each side and slightly pink in the center, about 8 minutes per side. An instant-read thermometer inserted into the center of each chop should read 145 degrees F (63 degrees C). Let rest for 5 minutes before serving.

Nutrition Facts (per serving)
- 756 Calories
- 42g Fat 
- 30g Carbs
- 62g Protein
