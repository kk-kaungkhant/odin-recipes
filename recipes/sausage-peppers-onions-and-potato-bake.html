Sausage, Peppers, Onions, and Potato Bake

Prep Time: 20 minutes

Cook Time: 40 minutes

Total Time: 1 hour

Servings: 8

Description

This sausage and potato bake is a classic Italian comfort food made with Italian sausage, potatoes, bell peppers, and onions. The ingredients are cooked together with white wine, chicken stock, and Italian seasoning, then baked until hot and bubbling.

Ingredients

  • 2 teaspoons olive oil
  • 2 pounds Italian sausage links, cut into 2-inch pieces
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 4 large potatoes, peeled and thickly sliced
  • 2 large green bell peppers, seeded and cut into wedges
  • 2 large red bell peppers, seeded and cut into wedges
  • 3 large onions, cut into wedges
  • 1/2 cup white wine
  • 1/2 cup chicken stock
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Preheat the oven to 400 degrees F (200 degrees C).
  3. Heat 2 teaspoons of olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Cook and stir the sausage until browned, about 5 to 10 minutes. Transfer the sausage to a large baking dish.
  4. Pour 1/4 cup of olive oil into the skillet. Cook the potatoes, stirring occasionally, until browned, about 10 minutes.
  5. Transfer the potatoes to the baking dish, leaving some oil in the skillet.
  6. Cook and stir the green and red peppers and onions in the skillet until they begin to soften, about 5 minutes.
  7. Add the vegetables to the baking dish. Pour the wine and chicken stock over the vegetables and sausage. Sprinkle with Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper.
  8. Gently stir the sausage, potatoes, and vegetables together.
  9. Bake in the preheated oven until hot and bubbling, about 20 to 25 minutes. Serve hot.
